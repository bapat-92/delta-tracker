Estimate = short lots × BTC per lot × spot ÷ leverage. Max profit and max loss use your entry prices and lots, assuming everything is held to expiry (position P&L at expiry is worked out from the strikes). It ignores margin scaling for large sizes, offsetting between legs and fees, and I haven't confirmed Delta applies this formula to short options. The order preview on Delta shows the real requirement, so use that.

Premium value is lots × BTC per lot × option price, in USD. You collect it when you sell; it rises as the options gain value. The trigger is the premium value at which your loss equals the stop-loss (entry value + stop-loss). With the net basis, bought legs are subtracted from both the entry and the current value. Based on mark prices, so it lags and doesn't place any order.

Lots are sized so the estimated margin (short lots × BTC per lot × spot ÷ leverage, the leverage box above) equals the margin share of your capital, split equally across the sells you added from the finder. The premium target is a share of capital you want to collect, and the premium figures use the entry prices in the table. Capital is in USD, and the margin estimate is rough, so check Delta's order preview before trading.
