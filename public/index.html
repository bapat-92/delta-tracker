Market features: profit days vs loss days (volume, IV, BTC moves before entry)

Run the backtest first (it gives the days, entry times, strikes and results). This then downloads 1-minute traded volume for the four strikes of each trade, BTC 5-minute volume and prices, and optionally open interest, and compares profit days with loss days over the 5 minutes, 15 minutes, 1 hour and 2 hours before entry. Features: BTC move, range and volume spike, option volume at the sold and bought strikes (and how many of your sold lots that covers), and implied volatility level and change (worked out from the mark prices). Click a loss day in the loss tables to see its volume strip under the chart. Large tables of past days take a few minutes to download the first time; later runs use the saved copy.

Download Historical Data

Downloads 1-minute option mark prices straight from Delta's public API (no key, nothing is traded) for every daily expiry in your date range, and saves them as zip files in the same format as "Export the saved candle data". A range longer than 2 months is split into 2-month files that are downloaded one after another; each file is saved as soon as it is finished. Keep this tab open while it runs.

Inverted Batman: best strike combinations

Sells more outer options, buys fewer inner ones (default 5,000 sold / 1,500 bought). Ranks strikes by the widest BTC range at expiry that keeps your minimum profit.

Uses the leverage and BTC-per-lot boxes above. Buys are priced at the ask and sells at the bid.
Position P&L over the last 24 hours

Hourly move study

Loads Delta's hourly BTC candles and shows the biggest one-hour moves over the period, so you can judge how far BTC can travel in an hour.

Not-inverted Batman: back-ratio spreads

Opposite of the Inverted Batman: sell fewer near, buy more far, so a big move pays. Ranks by profit if BTC stays, with the pocket loss inside your cap.

Uses the funds from the Inverted Batman box (or Available balance), and the leverage and BTC-per-lot boxes above.
